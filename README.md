# adapt

### An AI-Powered Adaptive Digital Assistance Platform

ADAPT is an intelligent accessibility platform that helps users complete everyday digital and real-world tasks by breaking complex procedures into simple steps and dynamically changing the way it interacts with the user based on their behaviour.

Instead of requiring users to adapt to technology, **ADAPT adapts technology to the user.**

---

## 🌟 The Problem

Many digital experiences assume that every user can:

- Easily understand complex instructions
- Process large amounts of information at once
- Type and navigate quickly
- Understand unfamiliar interfaces
- Recover from mistakes without assistance

For users experiencing cognitive overload, low digital literacy, or interaction difficulties, even simple everyday tasks can become overwhelming.

Traditional accessibility systems usually provide a fixed interface.

ADAPT takes a different approach:

> **It observes how a user interacts with a task and dynamically changes the level and form of support it provides.**

---

# 💡 What is ADAPT?

ADAPT is an AI-powered digital task assistant.

A user simply tells ADAPT what they want to accomplish.

For example:

> "I want to book a bus."

> "I need help doing my laundry."

> "I want to book a doctor's appointment."

ADAPT then:

1. Understands the user's goal.
2. Retrieves or generates a procedure.
3. Breaks the procedure into small, manageable steps.
4. Guides the user through one step at a time.
5. Monitors interaction behaviour.
6. Detects signs of difficulty or friction.
7. Dynamically changes its instructions, interaction method, and interface.
